Welcome to Eleven-J

Eleven-J teaches every concept of system design and networking through animation, backed by clear, descriptive explanations.

Each idea is shown moving first, so you can see how data flows, then explained in plain words, step by step, so it truly sticks.

Every concept, taught two ways

1Watch it

A short animation shows what happens, such as a request travelling across the network or a server taking load.

2Understand it

A descriptive explanation walks through the why and how in simple language, with no skipped steps.

3Apply it

Case studies and blog articles connect each concept to real systems you can reason about.

About the site

How you learn

Read articles and blog posts, follow case studies step by step, and watch animations that make tricky ideas easy to see.

What we cover

System design and networking, from the basics to real-world architecture.
